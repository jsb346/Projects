## Level One - Conversational Analytics and Critical Thinking in Baseball

### Course Overview

This course provides an introductory exploration of baseball data and analytics, including historical eras of analytics,
descriptive vs. predictive data, metrics vs. statistics, key hitting, pitching, and fielding terms and practical applications of
each, and player comparisons.

### Case Study Prompt

You are working for an MLB team. Your team wants to add one hitter and one pitcher. It has narrowed down its candidates to four players: two first basemen and two starting pitchers. Please advocate for one position player and one pitcher and explain your choice. 

Limit your answer to 1,500 words total, or approximately 750 words each for your hitter and pitcher choice using the open-ended prompts below. You can access data sets to help you with these comparison pieces, click on the Player Comparison Data tab above and select either Module 13 (Position Players) or 14 (Pitchers) to download the excel attachments there.

## Level Two - Advanced Analytics in Baseball

### Course Overview

Level Two provides a deeper study of the key concepts in baseball analytics — hitting, pitching, fielding, and baserunning metrics, park and league adjustments, replacement levels and WAR, as well as projections and economics — helping to develop critical thinking. The goal of this course is to prepare learners to apply this knowledge and analytical ability to their own research — including the case prompt at the end of the course — and to be equipped for the technical skills introduced in Level Three.
